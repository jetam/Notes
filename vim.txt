
		LEARNING VIM SHORTCUTS

Esc: go in normal mode at any time
i: insert mode

:w : save
:wq : save and quit
:q! : save without quitting

move with: h j k l
move by words: w b e
move to beginning / end of line: 0 $
move to beginning / end of file: gg G

delete: x
delete word: dw
delete line: dd
delete 3 lines: 3dd

undo: u
redo: ctrl + r

copy: yy
paste: p
paste above: P
copy 5 lines: 5yy

search: /hello
next: n
previous: N

replace 1 character: r
change word: cw
change line: cc
